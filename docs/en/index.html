What Muskl does

Everything you need to train seriously. Nothing more.

An app built to be open during your workout, not just after it.

🏋️

Detailed workouts

Exercises, sets, reps, weights and RPE. A catalog sorted by muscle group, or your own exercises.

⏱️

Live mode

Check off each set during your workout, with a rest timer that starts on its own.

📅

Calendar and streak

Your consistency at a glance, your weekly goal, and a way to add a session you forgot to log.

📈

Progress

Body weight chart and history per exercise: you can really see whether you're moving forward.

📋

Templates

Save a workout as a template and start it again in one tap. A starter program is waiting for you as soon as you sign up.

⚖️

Kilos or pounds

Pick your unit once and the whole app adapts. Detected automatically from your country.

Not just lifting

Logging adapts to the exercise.

Not every sport is counted in reps. Muskl knows a plank is timed and a bench press is loaded.

  • Reps and weight — bench press, squat, pull-ups.
  • Duration — plank, jump rope, rowing machine, assault bike.

Together

Training is easier together.

A feed that only shows workouts from people you follow. No strangers, no content pushed by an algorithm.

  • Follows — request to follow, or keep your profile public.
  • Likes and comments on your friends' workouts.
  • Shareable profile with a simple link.
  • Blocking and control over who sees your body stats.

Everywhere

Available in 7 languages.

Exercise names included, translated the way people actually say them at the gym — not word for word.
