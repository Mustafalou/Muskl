Open beta — iOS & Android

Install Muskl
in two minutes.

The app is being tested before its official release on the stores. How you install it depends on your phone.

iPhone

Through TestFlight, Apple's official testing app.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It's free and made by Apple.
  2. Open the Muskl invitation on your iPhone with the button below.
  3. Tap “Accept”, then “Install”. Muskl appears on your home screen.
1. Download TestFlight 2. Open the Muskl invitation

Updates arrive straight in TestFlight: you'll get a notification for every new version.

Android

Through a Google Play invitation.

  1. Leave your Gmail address below: the one of the Google account signed in to your phone's Play Store.
  2. I add you to the testers and email you your invitation link.
  3. Open the link on your phone, accept the invitation, then install Muskl from the Play Store.

Your address is only used to add you to the testers. It reaches me through the FormSubmit service. Privacy

During the beta, Muskl doesn't show up in Play Store search: always use your link. It may also appear under a technical name, “com.muskl.app”. And stay enrolled in the test for a few weeks — that's what lets Muskl launch officially on the Play Store.
