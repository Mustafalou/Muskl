Privacy policy — Muskl

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This is a translation of the French policy, which prevails in case of any difference.

Muskl is a workout tracking app (weightlifting, cardio and other sports) with a social side. This page explains what data is collected, how it's used, and how you can control or delete it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Muskl is a project developed and run independently. For any question, contact: mustafaliiyilmaz@gmail.com.

2. Data collected

  • Account: email address and password (handled by Supabase Auth, never stored in plain text).
  • Profile: username, profile photo (optional), “Public profile” setting.
  • Welcome questionnaire: your training level, used to suggest starter workout templates.
  • Preferences: weekly workout goal, weight unit (kg or lb).
  • Body stats (optional): height, weight history.
  • Workouts: name, date, notes, exercises, sets (reps, weight, duration, RPE) and your workout templates.
  • Social interactions: the accounts you follow and that follow you, your likes, your comments and the accounts you block.
  • Reports: if you report content, we record which workout and who reported it.

Muskl does not collect your location, your contacts or any advertising identifier.

On the website: if you request access to the Android beta through the form on the Install page, your email address reaches me through the FormSubmit service. It is only used to add you to the testers list and send you your invitation.

3. Who can see your data

Your username and profile photo are visible to other users, for example in search.

Your workouts (with their exercises and sets), your height and your weight history depend on the “Public profile” setting, in the Profile tab. When it's on (the default), any user can see them by opening your profile. When it's off, only people whose follow request you've accepted can see them.

The feed only shows each person the workouts of the people they follow. Likes and comments on a workout are visible to those who can see that workout. If you block a user, they can no longer see your workouts.

4. Data processor

Data is hosted by Supabase (PostgreSQL database, authentication, photo storage), in the European Union (eu-central-1 region). No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising — Muskl shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics tools.

5. Retention period

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted (see next section).

6. Deleting your account and your data

From the app: Profile tab → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app (for example if you've already uninstalled it): email mustafaliiyilmaz@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”, from the email address of your Muskl account. Deletion is carried out within 30 days at most and you'll receive a confirmation.

In both cases, deletion is permanent and covers all your data: your account, your profile and photo, your questionnaire answers and preferences, your workouts, exercises and sets, your workout templates, your weight history, your follows and followers, your likes, your comments, your blocks and your reports. Muskl keeps none of this data after deletion.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data. Most of these actions are available directly in the app (edit your profile, delete your account). For any other request, contact us at the address below.

8. Reporting content

You can report a workout from the feed. Reports are visible only to you and the Muskl team, never to other users.

9. Security

Access to data is protected by security rules applied to each row of the database (Row Level Security), ensuring that only the owner of a piece of data can change or delete it. Communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

10. Minimum age

Muskl is not intended for people under 16.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the last change is shown at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data:

mustafaliiyilmaz@gmail.com
